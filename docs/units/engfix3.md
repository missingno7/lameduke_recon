# December 1994 ENGINE matching pass

The fresh baseline from the integrated `src/engine/ENGINE.C` is 81 of 103 STRICT functions and 38,423 verified bytes. It has 22 NEAR functions and zero CODE_BYTES_MATCH functions. The canonical compiler is Watcom 9.5 GA under DOSBox-X with `-4r -s -or`. `wc95b` is the pinned install key. The status snapshot in `docs/STATUS.md` and the fifth-pass unit page predate this baseline. [Baseline](baseline.json) · [Baseline output](baseline.log)

## C inlining

- **PROVEN.** The standard build and oracle have the same 181 decoded direct calls between the 103 ENGINE publics, including identical multiplicities by caller and callee. NT probes with `-oe=10,20,40,90,150,400,800,1500,3000` change at least 4 and up to 150 caller/callee pairs. They lose between 12 and 52 existing STRICT functions. Ken's `buil1995/MAKEFILE:23` and `BUILDBAK/MAKEFILE:23` specify `wcc386 engine /4r /s /or`. `BUIL0422/MAKEFILE:11` specifies `/4r`. `SRC_9505/MAKEFILE:11` has a commented `/4s /s /or` recipe. Each of these uses the documented register optimization or its earlier setup, without `-oe`. [Call census and threshold results](inlining.json)
- **STRONG.** The two explicit signed cross products in the wall- and floor-sprite paths of `drawsprite` follow the written-out C in March 1995 (`ENGINE.C:3984,4275`), May 1995 (`:3818,4108`) and June 1995 (`:3839,4129`). Those donor bodies keep the commented `spritewallfront` call beside the cross product and handle the zero result with a sector test. The exported helper returns a Boolean, which loses that zero case. The face-sprite path and both `drawmasks` sites still call `spritewallfront` in D3D. The `-oe=90` probe removes all three observed calls. This separates the related wall tests from the expanded functions found in the game units.
- **STRONG.** A register-independent scan of every ENGINE public lacking an intra-ENGINE direct call compared runs of at least eight instructions with at least two exact LE relocation targets. The longer overlaps follow shared algorithms, including the 24-instruction wall range setup in `getzrange`/`getcliplines` and the 17-instruction sprite setup in `overwritesprite`/`permanentwritesprite`. The archive sources show those blocks written out. Several entries receive direct calls elsewhere in the executable, or are interrupt handler entries. The motif scan is diagnostic because source inlining can alter instruction scheduling and register lifetimes. [Cross-reference and motif results](orphan-motifs.json)

## Source trials and allocator checks

- **PROVEN.** `scansector` retains seven differing register-encoding bytes and verified fixups. The fresh `cg95` trace confirms DI for `point2` at `1:343e8`, with target AX rejected due to overlapping `t92` and `t88`. `semir` reports the shared return's EAX expression separately. The short, signed-long, unsigned-short, pointer and lifetime variants tested here did not beat seven bytes. [Trace explanation](traces/scansector-explain.json) · [Source forms](scansector-trials.json) · [Allocator forms](scansector-allocator-trials.json)
- **PROVEN.** `hline` retains eight differing stack-offset bytes. The validated trace shows `r` and `xr` with equal home priority and input ranks 52 and 53, respectively. Block scope, local copies and splitting the span arithmetic did not exchange the homes. `calcwalls` kept its ten stack-offset differences when tested with local or reordered numerator and sky tile declarations. [Trace explanation](traces/hline-explain.json) · [Hline forms](hline-allocator-trials.json) · [Calcwalls forms](calcwalls-allocator-trials.json)
- **PROVEN.** A VESA buffer named `vesa_rmi` and provisionally bound to `2:1bde9f` clears eight stock matcher target warnings. The oracle clears a 50-byte buffer at `1:474d2` and passes it through EDI to DPMI 0300h at `1:47539–47543`. The fields at +0x22, +0x1c and +0x18 identify ES, EAX, and ECX. In the public versions, `_vesa_rmi` lands at `_BSS+0x10b030`, which gives `2:1bce30` under the independently anchored BSS base. The historical function-local static in May/June and in the worker source emits the correct anonymous offset. The public version changes storage order despite clearing the function's relocation diagnostics. It remains a diagnostic. [VESA probe](rmi.log) · [Diagnostic overlay](rmi-overlay.json)
- **OPEN.** `rotatesprite` is the only remaining engine public with a baseline extent different from its independently bound successor. It emits 1,532 bytes against 1,497. The same 35-byte excess explains the shared epilogue differences in `setvesamode`, `setverts` and `setredbluepalette`. March's source is partial and later versions redesign the function. Dimension width, clip quotient, viewport coordinate and raster setup probes preserve the source alternatives in the trial records. [Rotation setup trials](rotatesprite-trials.json) · [Column trials](rotate_columns-trials.json)
- **NEGATIVE.** The `getcliplines` range and queue reshapes, and the `neartag` packed endpoint loads and coordinate ordering, did not beat their baselines while preserving all STRICT functions. Alternative forms in `wallfront` either regressed shared displacements or moved farther from the target. ## DOS candidate

`src/ENGINE.C` retains two same-extent improvements. `raytrace` names the first wall-direction deltas as shown by the reused products at `1:435ab–435e3`. Grouping the endpoint locals by axis also gives the correct parameter homes. `setview` spells out each stride calculation and preserves the final mode-7 top and bottom outputs at `1:463df–4640b`. The explicit multiply-by-five and shifts are compiler-steered source choices. All other function bytes and fixup results in the report equal the baseline. The two headers are exact copies of the originals.
